\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This survey has systematically charted the landscape of Structural Entropy
research over the decade since its introduction in 2016.
We have consolidated its theoretical underpinnings, computational
methodologies, and burgeoning applications across AI, presenting the field's
first comprehensive synthesis.

Our review reveals that SE offers a distinct and powerful lens for artificial
intelligence: it shifts the focus from local, pairwise interactions to the
global, hierarchical organization of complex systems.
We have detailed how minimizing SE serves as a constructive principle to
uncover a system's most efficient information-theoretic abstraction---a
capability that has been effectively harnessed in graph learning for pooling
and structure augmentation, in reinforcement learning for hierarchical
state--action abstraction and exploration, and in diverse application domains
from genomics to speech processing.

The reproducibility benchmark in Section~\ref{sec:benchmark} demonstrates
that differentiable SE objectives, as implemented in the \texttt{glass-jax}
library, match or exceed state-of-the-art methods on realistic, noisy graph
topologies while offering the key advantage of end-to-end gradient-based
optimization.

In conclusion, Structural Entropy provides more than a new set of tools: it
offers a principled way of thinking about information and structure in a
hierarchical world.
By providing a rigorous method to quantify and optimize organizational
uncertainty within data, SE holds the potential to guide the development of AI
systems that are not only more powerful but also more interpretable, robust,
and theoretically grounded.
The eight open problems identified in Section~\ref{sec:open} chart a research
agenda for the next decade.

\paragraph{Differences from the IJCAI-2025 Conference Version.}
This journal article substantially extends~\cite{su2025ijcai} in the following
ways:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item A new, self-contained \emph{formal foundations} section with precise
        definitions and comparative analysis against modularity, map equation,
        and von Neumann entropy.
  \item An extended \emph{algorithm catalogue} (Table~\ref{tab:algorithms})
        covering all known SE minimization methods with unified complexity
        notation.
  \item A new \emph{reproducibility benchmark} (Section~\ref{sec:benchmark})
        with empirical comparisons over multiple datasets and random seeds.
  \item Expanded \emph{application coverage}: speech processing (SECodec,
        SEVC), geoscience knowledge quantification (KQI), and updated
        2025--2026 literature.
  \item A structured \emph{open problems} section
        (Section~\ref{sec:open}) absent from the conference version.
  \item Release of \texttt{glass-jax} as a companion reproducibility library.
\end{enumerate}
