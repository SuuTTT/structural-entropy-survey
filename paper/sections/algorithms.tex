\section{Theoretical Foundations and Computational Algorithms}
\label{sec:theory}
\label{sec:algorithms}

\subsection{Foundational Theoretical Works}
\label{subsec:theory-foundation}

\paragraph{Foundation Theory.}
The concept of SE was introduced by~\cite{Li2016} as a principled,
information-theoretic framework for quantifying the complexity of a graph's
structure.
The seminal work defined SE as the minimum code length required to describe a
network's hierarchy under a random walk process, bridging graph theory with
Shannon's information principles.
It established that minimizing SE reveals a graph's intrinsic multi-scale
organization.
Building on this,~\cite{li2024book} significantly broadened the theoretical
landscape by situating SE within the \emph{mathematical principles of the
information world}, formulating fundamental information laws and the
Information Science Principles of AI (ISPAI), providing axiomatic foundations
for a comprehensive structural information theory.

\paragraph{Theoretical Analysis.}
Subsequent research has elucidated SE's properties and forged connections with
established graph-theoretic concepts.
The ``togetherness'' metric~\cite{ljm2021improvingTogetherness} uses SE
reduction to quantify the efficacy of network integration when adding
inter-community edges.
\cite{liu2022similaritygap} established a significant theoretical link between
SE and spectral graph theory: for any undirected graph, the difference between
SE and the von Neumann graph entropy (VNE) is bounded within the interval
$[0,\gamma]$, formally connecting SE to spectral properties of the graph
Laplacian.
\cite{yang2024incremental} proposed incremental computation paradigms for
dynamic graphs, enabling efficient encoding tree updates as the network evolves.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
The original formulation targets simple graphs; significant research has
extended SE to more complex network structures.
For directed graphs,~\cite{yaopan2019flowDirected} introduced a localized 2D
SE via a flow-based algorithm, while~\cite{wxb2024rwedirected} proposed a
random walk-based entropy (RWE) with a proven theoretical connection to VNE.
Multi-relational SE~\cite{cao2024multi} uses a random surfing model that
jointly optimizes node and relation selection.
The \emph{infected tree entropy} (ITE)~\cite{wxb2023findingThesource}
specializes SE for source localization in information cascades.
SE-guided probabilistic coding~\cite{huang2024structuralProbCoding}
regularizes embeddings in natural language understanding tasks.

\paragraph{Heuristic Methods.}
deDoc~\cite{li2018dedoc} pioneered SE minimization for genomic structure
detection with a greedy merging strategy.
SEAT~\cite{chen2022pyseat} adapts this for single-cell omics by applying SE
minimization on cell--cell graphs to reveal nested subpopulations.
HCSE~\cite{pan2021information} is a widely used, general-purpose hierarchical
clustering framework that recursively isolates the sparsest levels of a
hierarchy through SE-guided optimization, automatically determining hierarchy
depth without hyperparameters.

\paragraph{Constrained Optimization.}
SuperTAD~\cite{zhang2021supertad} combines interval dynamic programming with
SE minimization under \emph{spatial contiguity} constraints.
SuperTAD2~\cite{ling2024supertad2} introduces matrix discretization and
approximation strategies achieving ${\approx}10\times$ speedup.
deDoc2~\cite{li2023dedoc2} extends the contiguity approach to single-cell
analysis to resolve chromatin hierarchies across the cell cycle.
SSE~\cite{zeng2024semiSSE} unifies \emph{semi-supervised} constraints
(must-link / cannot-link pairwise constraints and label constraints) by
reformulating SE to penalize violations during encoding tree construction.
SSSE~\cite{zeng2024scalableSSSE} scales this to massive datasets via graph
sampling and incremental cluster insertion, reducing time complexity from
quadratic to nearly linear.

\paragraph{Differentiable / Gradient-Based Methods.}
LSENet~\cite{sun2024lsenet} creates a differentiable SE formulation in
hyperbolic space, enabling cluster-free hierarchical partitioning via
backpropagation.
DeSE~\cite{zhang2025dese} pioneers a \emph{soft assignment structural entropy}
allowing co-design of graph topology and cluster assignments in an
end-to-end model.
CoDeSEG~\cite{xian2025communitySEGame} offers a game-theoretic formulation
for near-linear-time overlapping community detection.

\paragraph{Complexity and Trade-off Analysis.}
Heuristic approaches offer scalability and generality but are limited in their
ability to incorporate domain knowledge.
Constrained methods integrate domain-specific knowledge at the cost of
broader applicability.
Differentiable methods bridge SE and deep learning but introduce
hyperparameter sensitivity and potential interpretability concerns.
A key open challenge is hybridizing these paradigms to achieve an optimal
balance of scalability, accuracy, and interpretability, especially for dynamic
or heterogeneous networks (see Section~\ref{sec:open}).
